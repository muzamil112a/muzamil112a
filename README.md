**What it does**
- 📄 **PDF Studio:** translate a PDF (keeps the layout, handles Urdu & Arabic right-to-left), merge, split, compress to a target size, protect, unlock, convert
- 🔳 **QR Studio:** Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, vCard, location and event QR codes with your colours and logo (PNG / JPG / SVG)
- 🖼️ **Photo tools:** photo to 20/50/100 KB for job forms, passport & CNIC photos on A4, background remover, OCR
- 🧾 **CV & invoices:** CV maker with an ATS check, invoices, quotations and receipts as PDF

**How it's built**
- PDF, QR and ZIP tools run **entirely in the browser**, so user files never leave the device
- Node.js + Express backend only where a server is needed (translation, video)
- Static, pre-rendered pages for speed and SEO, with Urdu, Roman Urdu, Arabic and Hindi versions
- Deployed on Vercel

## 🎙️ VocaTwin: AI voice cloning app (team lead)

**Founder & UI Lead** of VocaTwin, our final year project (BS Software Engineering). Record 30 seconds of your voice, clone it, scan your face and get a talking avatar, plus an AI chatbot, all in one Flutter app.

- 🎨 **My role:** app design, Flutter frontend, project management
- 🧠 **Inside:** zero-shot voice cloning (Coqui TTS · YourTTS), on-device face detection (Google ML Kit), DeepSeek R1 chatbot, Firebase auth & storage
